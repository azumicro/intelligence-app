Intelligence app: Privacy Policy

Intelligence is a personal desktop app that runs only on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses. When you sign in with Google, the app requests access to your Google Tasks, Google Calendar and Gmail so it can show, create, complete and organise your own tasks, events and emails inside the app.

Where data goes. Your Google data is read directly from Google's APIs onto your own computer and stays there. It is not sent to the app's developer, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Sign-in tokens are stored in your operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain).

Your control. You can disconnect at any time from the app's Settings, or revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
